Our mission

Health care built by, and for, our own communities.

The Northern California Native & Indigenous Health Professions Coalition unites Native American and Indigenous medical students at Stanford, UCSF, and UC Davis to strengthen Indigenous health and expand culturally grounded medical education.

Together, we build a supported pathway for Native undergraduates across Northern California to enter the health professions — connecting students at 17 partner campuses with mentors and community at our three member medical schools.

Programs

A pathway, not a single program.

We support students at every stage — from first exposure to a health career through to practicing in a tribal or community clinic.

01

Undergraduate Outreach

Connecting with Native and Indigenous undergraduates across our 17 partner campuses — from Cal Poly Humboldt to UC Merced — to introduce the path into medicine early and build community among pre-health students.

17 partner campuses
02

Pre-Health Support

Guidance, peer community, and application support for Native and Indigenous undergraduates preparing for medical school and other health professional programs.

Application support
03

Medical School & Mentorship

A community for Native American and Indigenous medical students at Stanford, UCSF, and UC Davis — supporting culturally grounded medical education and mentoring the next generation of undergraduates behind them.

3 member medical schools
04

Strengthening Indigenous Health

Graduates carry culturally grounded care and mentorship back into Native and Indigenous communities across Northern California — continuing the pathway for the students who come after them.
