Board & Founding Members

The people building the coalition.

Meet the founding members who established the coalition in 2026, and the board members who lead it each year after.

Founding Members

2026
LS

Landon Swopes

ANAMS President

UC Davis School of Medicine
Class of 2029

Tribal affiliation: Chickasaw Nation

Landon is a graduate of Stanford University, where he earned his Bachelor's degree in Biology, after college he received a Masters of Science in Law from Northwestern Pritzker School of Law.

OM

Oren Antonio Morgan

SAIMS Co-President

Stanford School of Medicine
Class of 2029

Tribal affiliation: Otomi

Oren Antonio Morgan is an MD candidate at Stanford University School of Medicine and co-chair of Stanford American Indigenous Medical Students, where he helped found the Northern California Native and Indigenous Health Professions Coalition and leads its governance and institutional partnership work

HS

Heather Shipley

SAIMS Co-President

Stanford School of Medicine
Class of 2030

Tribal affiliation: Paiute

Heather Shipley is a Paiute, first-generation college student and medical student at Stanford School of Medicine committed to advancing health equity, improving healthcare access, and strengthening pathways into medicine for Indigenous and underserved communities.

TB

Taylor Bhaiji

ANAMS Co-President

UCSF School of Medicine
Class of 2029

Tribal affiliation: Seneca

Taylor Bhaiji is a second-year medical student at UCSF. Taylor graduated from Columbia University with a psychology major. During her two gap years before medical school, Taylor worked in a lab at Columbia University Medical Center investigating how social risk factors—such as education, socioeconomic status, and discrimination—foster resilience or confer risk for Alzheimer's Disease and related disorders in non-Latinx Black and Latinx aging populations. Taylor has also devoted time in medical school to fostering leadership development among Native medical students.

LB

Leia Bonifacio

ANAMS Co-President

UCSF School of Medicine
Class of 2029

Tribal affiliation: Kumeyaay Inaja-Cosmit & Nahuatl

Leia Bonifacio earned her bachelor's degree from Tufts University, completed a Masters in Global Surgery at the University of Oxford, and her research at UCSF medical school focuses on improving access to surgical and trauma care for underserved populations, particularly American Indian and Alaska Native communities.

CC

Caroline Clement

ANAMS Co-President

UCSF School of Medicine
Class of 2029

Tribal affiliation: Choctaw Nation of Oklahoma

Before medical school, Caroline Clement earned her B.S. in Neurobiology from Stanford University and completed the Stanford Community Impact Fellowship at SIRUM, focused on medication access and patient-centered program development. Her current research spans pediatric neurology, medical education, and leadership development among Native medical students.

LH

Lindsey Hightower

ANAMS Co-President

UCSF School of Medicine
Class of 2030

Tribal affiliation: Ohkay Owingeh Pueblo

Lindsey earned her bachelors degree at Harvard and did pottery, dance, and Tewa language learning on her reservation (Ohkay Owingeh Pueblo) over her gap year. She is in the PRIME-US cohort at UCSF SOM and conducts research in emergency medicine and trauma surgery.

SS

Sita Strother

ANAMS Co-President

UCSF School of Medicine
MD-PhD, expected 2034

Tribal affiliation: Blackfeet Descendant

Sita grew up in Maryland and graduated from Yale University in 2020 with a B.A. in Anthropology and Global Health. She then worked in public health and emergency medicine in DC for 5 years. In her free time she coaches high school cross country, sings a cappella, and cares for her cat and chickens!

JW

Jacqueline Wright

ANAMS Co-President

UCSF School of Medicine
Class of 2029

Tribal affiliation: Cherokee Nation

Jacqueline Wright is a graduate of Duke University, where she earned her Bachelor's degree with distinction in Global Health and Neuroscience, and now researches Native representation in medical education, particularly within surgical specialties.
